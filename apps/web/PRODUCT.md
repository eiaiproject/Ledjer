# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Ledjer primarily speaks first to Indonesian UMKM owners, operators, and small finance or admin teams evaluating whether to move from spreadsheets, notebooks, or informal transaction tracking into structured bookkeeping. They need to understand quickly that Ledjer fits local business routines, records transactions correctly, and can produce reports they can trust.

The authenticated product then serves those same businesses in day-to-day accounting work: recording money in and money out (cash sales, expenses, transfers between cash and bank, owner deposits and withdrawals), watching balances, and producing standard financial reports without needing deep accounting software expertise.

## Product Purpose

Ledjer is a web-based bookkeeping and accounting platform for small and medium businesses in Indonesia. It turns everyday cash activity into balanced double-entry accounting and gives owners and staff a clearer view of cash and bank balances, revenue, expenses, and profit.

Success means a prospective customer understands the value from the landing page, signs up with confidence, completes setup quickly, and can keep accurate books without falling back to spreadsheets.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, grounded.

Ledjer should feel practical and locally relevant rather than abstractly financial. The voice should be direct, calm, and plain-spoken: confident about accounting correctness, careful with money language, and focused on helping business owners see what is happening in their business.

## Anti-references

Do not make Ledjer feel like cold enterprise fintech, generic gradient SaaS, childish bookkeeping software, or a decorative finance landing page that hides accounting correctness behind visual noise.

Avoid surfaces that look expensive for their own sake, use vague "business growth" promises without showing concrete workflows, or over-simplify accounting to the point that trust is weakened.

## Design Principles

Lead with the business outcome before the software feature.

Show accounting correctness as a trust signal, not as intimidating jargon.

Make local operational context visible: Indonesian UMKM, Rupiah, everyday sales, expenses, cash, and bank accounts.

Keep the landing page persuasive and concrete, then let the app interface become calm, dense, and task-focused.

Make financial status easy to scan without making money feel gamified.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, labels, keyboard access, and readable text. Support reduced motion. Use color-safe status patterns for success, warning, error, and info states so meaning is not carried by hue alone.

Because users may work with dense financial data for long sessions, prioritize legible typography, tabular numeric alignment, predictable navigation, forgiving forms, and clear error recovery.
